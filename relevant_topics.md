1) Cisco Relevance Profile

A regulation is potentially relevant if it touches any Cisco operational domain or exposure area.


A. Cisco Operational Domains

· Hardware manufacturing & sourcing

· Software & cloud services

· AI / data / networking and security technologies

· Supply Chain


B. Regulatory Exposure Areas

· Data protection & privacy

· Telecommunications

· Cybersecurity & critical infrastructure

· Trade, export controls, and sanctions

· Supply chain · Sustainability

· Labor & workforce regulations

· Financial reporting & tax

· Competition / antitrust

· AI & emerging tech governance


2) Regulation signals/definition

A. Regulation Types (High Signal)

· Laws, directives, regulations, acts

· Binding standards with enforcement (e.g., cybersecurity rules)

· Regulatory authority guidance with compliance expectations

B. Change Events (Critical)

· Newly enacted or passed (not enacted) regulation

· Amendments or revisions

· Enforcement dates announced

· Expanded scope or penalties
