2) Regulation signals/definition

A. Regulation Types (High Signal)

· Laws, directives, regulations, acts

· Binding standards with enforcement (e.g., cybersecurity rules)

· Regulatory authority guidance with compliance expectations

B. Change Events (Critical)

· Newly enacted or passed (not enacted) regulation

· Amendments or revisions

· Enforcement dates announced

· Expanded scope or penalties


Ignore commentary. Prioritize binding or soon-to-be-binding rules. For every source go to the site and find relevant news from the last 7 days

utilize the following sources for regulatory intelligence:

A. United States · Congress.Gov https://www.congress.gov/bill/

Example: Text - H.R.633 - 119th Congress (2025-2026): TAKE IT DOWN Act | Congress.gov | Library of Congress

· Federal Register (https://www.federalregister.gov) — official daily publication for rules, proposed rules, and notices.

· Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) — codified general and permanent rules.

· Agency sites:

o FTC (consumer protection, privacy, online safety)

o FCC (telecom, spectrum, online services)

o NIST (standards, cybersecurity frameworks)

o FDA (medical devices, software as a medical device, labeling)

B. European Union · EUR Lex (https://eur-lex.europa.eu) — treaties, directives, regulations, case law. Example: · Directive - 2022/2555 - EN - EUR-Lex

· European Commission “Have Your Say” (public consultations, upcoming initiatives).

· ENISA (cybersecurity).

· European Data Protection Board (EDPB) — GDPR guidance.

C. United Kingdom

· legislation.gov.uk — statutes, statutory instruments.

· Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) — data protection.

· Ofcom — telecoms, online safety.

· FCA — financial services regulation.


D. Canada

· Canada Gazette — official rules & notices.

· ISED (Innovation, Science & Economic Development) — telecom, spectrum, tech.

· Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) — privacy, data.


E. Asia-Pacific

· Singapore: IMDA (Infocomm Media Development Authority), PDPC (privacy).

· Japan: MIC (telecom), PPC (privacy commission).
